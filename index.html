How I build kits

Made to be used, not just admired

A kit is only as good as the file a buyer opens. These are the rules I hold every kit to.

Variables everywhere

Fills, strokes, padding, gaps and radii are bound to tokens. Change the brand color once and every screen follows.

Auto Layout, all the way down

Components resize with their content. Text never clips, cards never overlap, and screens adapt when copy changes.

Real states, real content

Default, hover, pressed, disabled and loading for every control, plus empty, error and offline screens. No lorem ipsum.
